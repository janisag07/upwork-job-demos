Pipedrive CRM implementation

Two connected pipelines for a construction relationship-to-bid workflow

This workspace maps how a commercial construction team moves an account from first contact to a qualified opportunity, then carries that opportunity through estimating and award — while the backlog and active-project tabs stay in the existing spreadsheet until there is a real reason to move them.

    Spreadsheet coexistence

    Not everything moves to Pipedrive on day one. Two tabs stay exactly where they are until the pipelines above have proven themselves.

    Sheet tab: Backlog

    Stays in the spreadsheet

    Long-running backlog reporting already works for the team and isn't blocking anything. Moving it before the pipelines are trusted would just create a second source of truth.

    Sheet tab: Active Projects

    Stays in the spreadsheet

    Active-project tracking has its own reporting shape the office already relies on. It migrates later, once relationship and opportunity data is clean and the team is comfortable in Pipedrive.

    Microsoft, email & security discovery

    • Confirm Microsoft 365 tenant admin access and who can approve a new connected app.
    • Check mailbox permissions needed for two-way email sync per user (3 initial seats).
    • Confirm whether conditional access or SSO policy restricts third-party app connections.
    • Agree on a shared inbox vs. individual sync approach before turning on email tracking.

    Training & handoff

    • One recorded walkthrough per pipeline, under 10 minutes each.
    • A one-page quick reference per stage: what belongs here, what to do next.
    • Two live training sessions for the three initial users, scheduled around field/site availability.
    • A shared runbook document covering field mapping, import decisions, and known limitations.

    This workspace is built to be delivered under white-label coordination with Accredited AI — client-facing communication and handoff documents follow their process, not a third-party brand.

    The safe first step

    Milestone 1 of the $2,000 engagement

    Discovery, field map, and a 25-row test migration

    1. Discovery call: walk the live spreadsheet tab by tab with the team.
    2. Field map: document exactly how each column becomes a Pipedrive field (see mapping table above).
    3. Build both pipelines with the exact stage labels used today, not generic CRM defaults.
    4. Run a 25-row test migration and review every accepted and rejected row with the client.
    Acceptance criteria before committing to a full migration Field map signed off by the client; test batch accepted/rejected counts reviewed together; duplicate and missing-data handling agreed in writing; backlog and active-project tabs explicitly confirmed to stay in Sheets for this phase.
